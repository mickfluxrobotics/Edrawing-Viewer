eDrawings ViewereDrawings, STEP, STL, 3MF, DXF and DWG (version 12)

Open a CAD file

Open eDrawings (.easm, .eprt), STEP, STL and 3MF models in 3D, or DXF and DWG drawings in 2D. Hide parts from the tree and take measurements. Files are read on this device and never uploaded.

Or drop the file here.

Controls

Menus

File
Open file, recent files and Close file.
View
Zoom to fit, standard views, projection, display style, saved views, and for some files the File units and Up axis.
Tools
Measure, Section, Mass and Paint area stay on the toolbar. Export holds snapshots, reports, parts lists, STL, DXF and printing.
Settings
The gear icon: measurement units, rotation style (Free or Turntable) and reverse scroll zoom.
Narrow windows
The tools fold into a More menu.

Mouse

Select
Click a part. Shift + click adds or removes parts. Drag a box left to right to take parts fully inside it, or right to left to take everything it touches. Shift + drag adds to the selection.
Right-click
Opens a menu for what’s under the pointer: Hide, Isolate, Zoom to, Make transparent, Measure from here, Show all, Undo and Redo. Right-clicking a name in the tree works too. Right-drag still pans.
Rotate
Middle-drag, or Alt + left-drag on a touchpad. Start the drag on the model and it turns about that point. With Rotation set to Free (in Settings, the gear icon) you can tumble the model all the way round; Turntable keeps it upright. The view cube or a standard view sets it upright again.
Pan
Ctrl + drag, or right-drag.
Zoom
Scroll forward to zoom in, at the pointer. Shift + middle-drag also zooms.
Zoom to fit
Double-click the mouse wheel, press F over empty space, use the Fit icon under the view cube, or View, Zoom to fit.

Hiding parts

Hide
H, the Hide button or the right-click menu. Untick names in the tree to hide whole assemblies.
Transparent
T, or Make transparent in the right-click menu, to see through parts without hiding them. Clicks pass through transparent parts to whatever is behind. Select them again and press T to make them solid.
Undo, redo
Ctrl + Z undoes the last hide, isolate, show all, tree tick, transparency change or restored view, up to 50 steps. Ctrl + Y (or Ctrl + Shift + Z) redoes. The arrow buttons above the tree do the same.
Show all
Shows everything again and makes every part solid.
Tree
Moving over a name lights up that part in the model.

Views

View cube
Top right. Click a face for a standard view, or an edge or corner for the views between them. The icon below it zooms to fit.
Normal to
F turns the view square-on to the flat face under the pointer, or to the last flat face picked in Measure. With no flat face, it snaps to the nearest standard view.
Standard views
View, Standard view, or Ctrl + 1 Front, 2 Back, 3 Left, 4 Right, 5 Top, 6 Bottom, 7 Isometric (in the installed app).
Section
Section cuts the model with a plane: pick Front, Top or Right, or a flat face you picked in Measure, then drag the slider through the model. Flip keeps the other side. The cut is shown hollow, and you can’t select what the section removes.
Saved views
View, Saved views stores the camera angle with what’s hidden and transparent, under a name, for this file on this device. Click a name to go back to it (Ctrl + Z returns to how things were).
Display
View, Display: Shaded with edges, Shaded, Hidden lines removed or Wireframe.
Projection
View, Projection: Orthographic (the SolidWorks default) or Perspective.

Measure

Measure
M, then move over the model: corners, edges and faces light up before you click. Two picks give the distance or angle between them. Esc clears; press it again to leave Measure.
Copy
The Copy button, or Ctrl + C, copies just the number, ready to paste: for example 381.00 for a hole of Ø 381.00 mm. A single point copies its X, Y and Z.
Drawing snaps
In DXF and DWG drawings the pointer snaps to end points (diamond), midpoints (triangle, when you’re close), circle and arc centres (ring) and intersections (cross).
Mass
Choose the units at the bottom of the panel: volume in mm³, litres or m³, area in mm² or m², mass in g or kg (Auto follows the toolbar units). Mass properties saved in an eDrawings file for the whole model, and volume with an estimated mass for selected parts. For STEP, STL and 3MF everything is estimated from the geometry. In drawings this button is Properties: size and total line length (the cut length of a flat pattern).

Paint area and coating report

Paint area
Works out the outside surface area, the area a coater would paint. Select the weldments or parts to include first (otherwise everything visible is used), then press Calculate. Counted surfaces turn green and everything else grey.
What isn’t counted
Surfaces inside closed tubes, faces pressed together at joints and gaps narrower than the gap setting. Hollow tubes with openings up to the size you set (110 × 110 mm to start with) are capped (shown in orange) unless shorter than the minimum length (150 mm), so their insides aren’t counted.
Accuracy
Fast, Normal or Fine sets the grid used to decide what’s inside and outside. Areas always come from the model’s real surfaces. Big selections use a coarser grid automatically, and the panel says how big a gap that closes.
Coating report
From the Paint area panel or Export: a PDF schedule for your coater with each item’s quantity, material, outside size, area and estimated weight, a picture of each item with the coated surfaces in green, and how the area was worked out. Choose materials in the report window; they’re remembered by part name. A CSV of the same table is there too.

Export

Snapshot
Export, Snapshot with markup: draw arrows, freehand lines and notes on the current view, then save it as a PNG or copy it to paste into an email or chat. Ctrl + Z removes the last mark.
Parts list
Export, Parts list: a CSV of every part with quantities and the assemblies it’s used in, all parts or only the visible ones. Copy parts list puts it on the clipboard for pasting straight into a spreadsheet.
Face to DXF
Right-click a flat face and choose Export face as DXF, or pick it in Measure and press Export DXF. You get a 1:1 outline in mm with holes and slots, using true lines, arcs and circles, ready for laser or plasma cutting.
STL
Right-click a part in the model or the tree and choose Export STL, or use Export, STL of selected parts or STL of everything visible. Hidden parts are left out. STL files are in millimetres.
Print
Drawings only: Export, Print or save as PDF. Choose paper, orientation, scale (fit to page or 1:1 to 1:100), black or coloured lines, and the whole drawing or the current view.

Files

Close
File, Close file closes the file and frees its memory.
Parts list width
Drag the edge of the parts list to make it wider or narrower. Double-click the edge to reset it.
File types
eDrawings (.easm, .eprt), STEP (.step, .stp), STL and 3MF open in 3D; DXF and DWG open flat, in 2D. STEP and DWG are converted on this device first; if the converter stumbles, the viewer tries once more by itself. STL has no units, so set File units in the View menu. If a model lies on its back, switch Up between Z and Y in the View menu.
Recent files
File menu, or the list on the start screen, reopens files with one click. This works in Chrome, for files opened with Open file, dragged in, or opened from the Files app. Chrome may ask for permission the first time. If a device can’t reopen saved files, the viewer switches this off and tells you.
Drawings
The tree lists layers. Click, Shift + click or drag a box to select lines, arcs and text. Middle-drag pans and scroll zooms. DWG files from AutoCAD R13 to 2018 are supported.

Units, rotation style and scroll direction are in Settings (the gear icon).
