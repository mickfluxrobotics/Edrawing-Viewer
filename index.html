eDrawings ViewerOpens .easm and .eprt files on this device (version 6)

Open an eDrawings file

Pick an .easm assembly or .eprt part to view it in 3D, hide parts from the tree and take measurements. The file is read on this device and never uploaded.

Or drop the file here.

Controls

Select
Click a part. Shift + click adds or removes parts. Drag a box left to right to select parts fully inside it, or right to left to select everything it touches. Shift + drag adds to the selection.
Hide
H hides the selected parts. Show all brings them back.
Rotate
Middle-drag, or Alt + left-drag on a touchpad
Pan
Ctrl + drag, or right-drag
Zoom
Scroll forward to zoom in, at the pointer. Shift + middle-drag also zooms.
Fit
F
Projection
Orthographic (SolidWorks default) or Perspective, from the menu in the toolbar
Measure
M, then click corners, edges or faces. Two picks give the distance or angle between them.
Mass
Shows the mass properties saved in the file for the whole model, and volume with an estimated mass for selected parts.
Clear
Esc clears every selection. Press it again to leave Measure.
Views
Ctrl + 1 Front, 2 Back, 3 Left, 4 Right, 5 Top, 6 Bottom, 7 Isometric (in the installed app)
